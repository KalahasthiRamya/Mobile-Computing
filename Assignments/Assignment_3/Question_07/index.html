🎓 Stage 5: Viva Cross-Defence Examination Prep

Student A Cross-Question:
"Why was AODV selected over DSDV for this disaster emergency network?"
Answer: Disaster nodes move unpredictably across collapsed terrain. DSDV would consume over 70% of scarce channel bandwidth simply broadcasting route tables for broken links. AODV discovers routes purely on demand, saving battery and radio capacity.
Student B Cross-Question:
"How does Mobile IP maintain connectivity when a mobile node changes its point of attachment?"
Answer: The mobile node retains its permanent Home Address for application sockets. When arriving at a new foreign router, it obtains a temporary Care-of Address (CoA) and registers it with its Home Agent, which encapsulates incoming packets in an IP-in-IP tunnel.
