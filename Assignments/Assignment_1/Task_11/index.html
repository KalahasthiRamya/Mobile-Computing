🏆 Recommended Engineering Architecture

Selected Strategy: 3-Sector 120° Frequency Reuse with Cluster Size N = 4, Cell Radius R = 0.85 km, coupled with on-demand Selective Microcell Splitting.
Design Parameter Recommended Specification Engineering Justification
Cluster Size (N) N = 4 ($i=2, j=0$) Yields 84 channels/cluster (28 chan/sector), maximizing trunking capacity compared to N=7.
Sectoring Angle 120° directional Reduces interferers from 6 to 2, boosting worst-case SIR to $>17.8 ext{ dB}$ (well above 15 dB threshold).
Initial Macro Sites 7 Base Stations ($R = 0.85 ext{ km}$) Fully covers 12 km² with contiguous overlap at minimal initial CAPEX ($C_{sites} \approx \$350,000$).
Scalability to 100k+ Selective Cell Splitting Subdivide only dense hostel blocks ($k=2$, $R=210 ext{ m}$) while leaving open campus at macro level.

Final Defense: Choosing $N=7$ would give excessive SIR ($>23 ext{ dB}$) at the cost of crippling trunking efficiency (only 16 channels/sector, causing premature blocking at 12,000 subs). $N=4$ with 120° sectoring strikes the optimal Pareto equilibrium between interference margin and traffic handling.
