🔬 Detailed Worst-Case Mathematical Derivation

In the worst-case corner, the mobile is at distance $R$ from its serving base station and at varying distances from the 6 co-channel base stations:
• 2 nearest co-channel interferers are at distance: $D_1 = D - R$
• 2 co-channel interferers are at distance: $D_2 = D$
• 2 furthest co-channel interferers are at distance: $D_3 = D + R$
$$\left( rac{S}{I} ight)_{worst} = rac{R^{-n}}{2(D - R)^{-n} + 2D^{-n} + 2(D + R)^{-n}} = rac{1}{2(Q - 1)^{-n} + 2Q^{-n} + 2(Q + 1)^{-n}}$$ For $N=7, n=4$ ($Q = 4.58$):
$D_1 = 3.58 R \implies (3.58)^{-4} = 0.00609$
$D_2 = 4.58 R \implies (4.58)^{-4} = 0.00227$
$D_3 = 5.58 R \implies (5.58)^{-4} = 0.00103$
$\sum I_i = 2(0.00609 + 0.00227 + 0.00103) = 0.01878 \implies SIR_{worst} = rac{1}{0.01878} = 53.25 = \mathbf{17.26 ext{ dB}}$ (vs $18.7 ext{ dB}$ under average equidistant approximation).
