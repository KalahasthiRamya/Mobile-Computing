🔄 Cell Splitting Recurrence Formulation

Geometric Progression ($R o R/2$):
When an overloaded cell of radius $R_{k-1}$ is split into smaller microcells of radius $R_k = R_{k-1}/2$, the new cell area is:
$$A_k = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} (R_{k-1}/2)^2 = \frac{1}{4} A_{k-1}$$ Hence, exactly 4 child micro-cells fit into the footprint of 1 parent cell.
Recurrences for Uniform Splitting:
• Number of cells: $N_c(k) = 4 \cdot N_c(k-1), \quad N_c(0) = N_0$
• Total system channels / capacity: $C(k) = 4 \cdot C(k-1) = C_0 \cdot 4^k$
• Required BS transmit power: $P_k = \frac{P_{k-1}}{2^n} = P_0 \cdot 2^{-n k}$
Selective Splitting Recurrence:
If only a fraction $\alpha \in [0, 1]$ of cells are overloaded:
$$N_c(k) = N_c(k-1) + 3\alpha \cdot N_c(k-1) = (1 + 3\alpha) \cdot N_c(k-1)$$
