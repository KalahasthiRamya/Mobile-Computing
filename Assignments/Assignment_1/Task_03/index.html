📐 Mathematical Derivations Step-by-Step

1. Hexagonal Geometry & Township Coverage:
Hexagon cell area: $A_{cell} = \frac{3\sqrt{3}}{2} R^2 \approx 2.5981 \cdot R^2\text{ km}^2$.
For township area $A_{town} = 12\text{ km}^2$, total cells required:
$$N_c = \left\lceil \frac{12}{2.5981 \cdot R^2} \right\rceil$$
2. Frequency Reuse Distance & Ratio:
Reuse distance between co-channel cell centers:
$$D = R\sqrt{3N}, \quad Q = \frac{D}{R} = \sqrt{3N}$$
3. Channels & Traffic per Cell (Erlang-B at 2% GOS):
Total channels $S = 336$. Channels per cell $C = \lfloor S / N \rfloor$.
Blocking probability given offered load $A$ Erlangs:
$$B(C, A) = \frac{\frac{A^C}{C!}}{\sum_{k=0}^C \frac{A^k}{k!}} = 0.02$$ Subscribers per cell: $M_{cell} = \frac{A}{0.025}$. Total system subscribers $M_{sys} = N_c \cdot M_{cell}$.
4. Co-Channel SIR Expression:
$$SIR = \frac{P_r}{\sum_{i=1}^{M_I} I_i} = \frac{R^{-n}}{M_I \cdot D^{-n}} = \frac{1}{M_I}\left(\frac{D}{R}\right)^n = \frac{(\sqrt{3N})^n}{M_I}$$ In decibels: $SIR_{dB} = 10 \log_{10}\left(\frac{(\sqrt{3N})^n}{M_I}\right)$.
