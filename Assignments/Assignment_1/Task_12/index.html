Task 12: Day-4 Design Challenge - Convocation / Sports Event

Managing a 60x traffic density surge (25,000 attendees, 1,250 Erlang) in a 1 km² stadium zone

⚡ DAY-4 DESIGN CHALLENGE (8 MARKS)

🚨 Section A: Impact Analysis (2 Marks)

Changed Requirement: Convocation / Sports Event brings 25,000 attendees generating 0.05 Erlang each into a 1 km² stadium zone for 4 hours, occurring twice monthly.

• Offered Event Traffic: $25,000 \times 0.05 = \mathbf{1,250\text{ Erlangs}}$ in 1 km²!
• Baseline Township Density: $10,000 \times 0.025 / 12 = 20.8\text{ Erlang/km}^2$.
• Traffic Density Jump: From $20.8\text{ E/km}^2$ to $1,250\text{ E/km}^2$ — an unprecedented 60x traffic surge!
• Violated Assumptions: Uniform spatial traffic distribution fails completely; permanent macrocell capacity planning cannot absorb this localized burst without massive over-provisioning.

💥 Section B: Evaluation of Original Design (2 Marks)

Behavior of Original Design under Surge:
• In the 1 km² stadium zone, the macro design provides only 1 base station sector (28 channels).
• 28 channels at 2% GOS can carry at most 18.6 Erlangs.
• Under 1,250 Erlang offered load, the blocking probability calculated via Erlang-B:
$$B(28, 1250) = \mathbf{97.7\%\text{ Call Blocking!}}$$ • Catastrophic Failure: The original network collapses immediately in the stadium zone, creating total outage and signaling channel saturation.

🛠️ Section C: Adaptation & Redesign Strategy (2 Marks)

Decision: ADAPT & MODIFY (Hybrid Temporary Architecture)
Permanent civil reconstruction of the township is unwarranted for a 4-hour event twice a month. Instead, deploy:

1. Four Temporary Cells-on-Wheels (COWs):
Parked around stadium perimeter with $60^\circ$ narrow-beam micro-antennas ($P_{tx} = 2\text{ Watts}$, $R = 180\text{ m}$).

2. Dynamic Channel Borrowing (DCB):
Since academic blocks are vacant on event days, borrow 160 duplex channels from academic sectors and allocate them exclusively to the stadium COWs.

3. Wi-Fi 6 High-Density Offload:
Offload 65% of data/messaging traffic to stadium Wi-Fi APs, leaving 437 Erlangs for cellular voice.
