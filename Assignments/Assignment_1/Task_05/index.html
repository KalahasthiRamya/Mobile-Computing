📈 Scaling Proportionalities & Growth Analysis

Core Scaling Relationships:
• Capacity per cell: $C \propto \frac{1}{N}$
• Signal-to-Interference Ratio: $SIR \propto N^{n/2}$
• Number of cells covering 12 km²: $N_c \propto \frac{1}{R^2}$
• Total system capacity: $Cap_{sys} \propto \frac{N_c}{N} \propto \frac{1}{R^2 N}$

⚖️ Break-Even Scale Interpretation

Break-Even Scale Analysis:
Up to $pprox 18,500$ subscribers, the 12 km² township can be sustained by a macro-cellular architecture ($R = 0.8 ext{ km}$, $N_c = 8$ sites, $120^\circ$ sectoring with $N=4$ or $N=7$).
Beyond 20,000 subscribers, increasing the number of macro sites causes cell overlap and excessive interference in open campus conditions ($n=2.5$).
Therefore, the break-even scale occurs at $pprox 18,500$ subscribers, beyond which hierarchical microcells and repeated cell splitting ($R o R/2$) become mandatory.
