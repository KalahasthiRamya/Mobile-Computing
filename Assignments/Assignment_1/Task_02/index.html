🎯 Dominant Governing Design Parameters

Which single quantity most strongly controls capacity and interference for each strategy, and why should the analysis be built around it?

Strategy Dominant Parameter Control Role & Capacity/SIR Impact
Fixed Cluster Reuse Cluster Size N Controls reuse ratio $Q = \sqrt{3N}$. Dictates channels per cell ($C = 336/N$) and SIR ($SIR \propto N^{n/2}$).
Sectorised Reuse Sectoring Angle β Reduces number of co-channel interferers $M_I$ ($360^\circ o 6$, $120^\circ o 2$, $60^\circ o 1$). Allows lower $N$.
Dynamic Channel Borrowing Borrowing Threshold θ Controls when idle channels are borrowed from adjacent tiers without causing carrier lockouts.
Repeated Cell Splitting Cell Radius R Controls cell area $A \propto R^2$ and total cells $N_c \propto 1/R^2$. Multiplies total system capacity by $4^k$.

🔍 Engineering Interpretation: Why Build Analysis Around Governing Parameter?

Mathematical Relationship:
Offered Traffic $A_{total} = M imes 0.025 ext{ Erlang}$. For $M = 1,000$, $A_{total} = 25 ext{ Erlang}$; easily served by 1 macro-cluster without splitting.
For $M = 100,000$, $A_{total} = 2,500 ext{ Erlang}$. Since the total spectrum is strictly fixed at 336 channels (yielding at most $pprox 315$ Erlangs per cluster), neither N nor sectoring can support 2,500 Erlang in 12 km² alone.
Therefore, Cell Radius R becomes the absolute governing design parameter, driving geometric area reduction via $A_{cell} \propto R^2$ and cell density $N_c \propto 1/R^2$.
