📝 Closed-Form Solution by Iteration / Substitution

Derivation of Minimum Rounds k:
Let initial system capacity be $C_0$ Erlangs and target subscriber traffic demand be $D$ Erlangs.
From Task 6, each round of uniform splitting multiplies capacity by 4:
$$C(k) = 4^k \cdot C_0$$ To satisfy demand $C(k) \ge D$:
$$4^k \cdot C_0 \ge D \implies 4^k \ge \frac{D}{C_0} \implies k \ge \log_4\left(\frac{D}{C_0}\right)$$ Since splitting rounds must be integer values:
$$k = \left\lceil \log_4\left(\frac{D}{C_0}\right) \right\rceil = \left\lceil \frac{\ln(D / C_0)}{\ln 4} \right\rceil$$
Selective Splitting Closed Form:
For selective splitting with overload fraction $\alpha$:
$$k_{sel} = \left\lceil \log_{1 + 3\alpha}\left(\frac{D}{C_0}\right) \right\rceil$$

As radius shrinks ($R_k = R_0 / 2^k$), the cell boundary crossing rate scales as $H_k \propto \frac{v}{R_k} = 2^k \cdot H_0$.
