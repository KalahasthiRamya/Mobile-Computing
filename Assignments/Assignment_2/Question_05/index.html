🛑 Practical Limits & Toll Plaza Design Decision

Design Decision: At a highway toll plaza, most vehicles arrive from the same angular direction. Would you rely on SDMA?
Verdict: NO, SDMA FAILS COMPLETELY.
• When two vehicles travel within the array's beamwidth (e.g. angular separation $< 10^\circ$), they fall into the same mainlobe. The array cannot synthesize a null between them without distorting the serving beam.
• In addition, metal toll booths and queuing trucks create heavy angular spread and rich multipath scattering, rendering spatial beam steering useless.
• Solution: Rely on TDMA or OFDMA time/frequency slotting at toll plazas rather than SDMA.
