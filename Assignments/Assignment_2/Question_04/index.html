🎓 Design Decision: Lecture Hall Guaranteed Rate

Inquiry: Why does maximizing total throughput starve some users?
Pure opportunistic best-subcarrier allocation allocates each subcarrier to the user with the instantaneous highest channel gain. Users experiencing deep shadow fading across all subcarriers receive zero assignments, driving Jain's Fairness Index down.

Lecture Hall Choice: Proportional Fair (PF) or Interleaved with Minimum Quota:
In a lecture hall exam or e-learning environment, every student must receive at least 10 Mbps for slide/video streaming. Therefore, an interleaved round-robin baseline plus opportunistic channel swapping guarantees zero starvation ($J \ge 0.96$) while capturing multi-user diversity gain.
