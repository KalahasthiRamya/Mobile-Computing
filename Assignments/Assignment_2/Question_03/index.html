Notice: Under ideal synchronization and equal power, Walsh codes achieve zero cross-talk ($ ext{BER}=0$). However, when one user transmits at 10x power or chip timing shifts, orthogonality collapses!

🔬 Deep Technical Inquiry

1. Why Walsh Codes Fail Unsynchronized:
Walsh codes satisfy $\sum_{i=1}^L W_j(i) W_k(i) = 0$ strictly at zero time shift ($ au = 0$). If users transmit asynchronously with fractional chip delay $ au e 0$, the partial cross-correlation is non-zero, destroying orthogonality.
2. Why Power Control is Essential:
In CDMA, all users share the exact same frequency band simultaneously. If User $B$ is close to the base station and transmits at $10 imes$ power, its cross-correlation sidelobes overwhelm User $A$'s despread signal, creating the fatal Near-Far Blockout.
